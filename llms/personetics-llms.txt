# Personetics

> Personetics is a Cognitive Banking platform that helps banks and financial institutions deliver AI-driven, financial-data-personalized customer engagement and money management. It analyzes customer transaction data in real time to surface personalized insights, proactive financial guidance, automated savings and cash-flow programs, and self-driving finance experiences embedded inside a bank's own mobile and web channels. Personetics serves hundreds of banks across roughly 30 global markets, reaching an estimated 150 million banking customers. The platform is delivered as enterprise software integrated into a bank's digital banking stack rather than as a public self-serve developer API.
